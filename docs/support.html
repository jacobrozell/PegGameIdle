Support

Peg Game Idle is a cozy idle take on the classic triangle peg solitaire puzzle. Jump pegs to earn Peg Points, upgrade your idle Auto-Jumper, prestige for permanent multipliers, and tackle a new Daily Puzzle each day.

How to play

  1. Tap a peg, then tap an empty landing hole two spaces away (or drag peg → hole).
  2. Each jump earns Peg Points. Finish the board for a completion bonus based on pegs left.
  3. When stuck, the board auto-deals a fresh one. Use Undo or Hint on the Play tab.
  4. Spend Peg Points on Upgrades. Prestige when the meter fills for a permanent global multiplier.
  5. Open Daily for today's shared puzzle — rewards prestige progress, not spendable points.

Common questions

Where is my progress stored?

On your device only. Use Settings → Export save JSON to back up manually.

I lost progress after reinstalling

Reinstalling without a backup removes local data. Restore from an exported save via Settings → Import save, or from an iCloud/device backup if you had one.

Offline earnings

While away, the Auto-Jumper keeps earning up to your Offline Reserve cap. Open the app to collect the welcome-back summary.
