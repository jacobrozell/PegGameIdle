Privacy Policy

Peg Game Idle is developed by Jacob Rozell. The app is local-first: your progress stays on your device. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics in version 1.0.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to our servers. There are no accounts and no third-party analytics SDKs in the current release.

What stays on your device

  • Game state — Peg Points, upgrades, prestige, streaks, daily progress, achievements, and board progress.
  • Settings — haptics, sound, ambient motion, and color theme preferences.

Data is stored in on-device storage (UserDefaults). It may be included in your device backups (iCloud or encrypted local backup) under Apple's control, not ours. We do not operate cloud sync.

Export and import

You can copy your save as JSON from Settings → Save Data → Export save JSON, or paste JSON to restore progress on the same device. You choose when to share that file.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset All Progress to wipe local progress, or delete the app to remove its data from your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy before or after App Store release. The date below reflects the latest revision.
